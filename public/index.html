Microphone Access Required

VoxBridge needs microphone permissions to capture your speech and translate it in real-time.

How to allow microphone access:
  1. Click the lock / settings icon 🔒 next to the web address bar at the top of your browser.
  2. Change the Microphone permission from Blocked to Allow.
  3. If you are using AI Studio inside an iFrame, click the Shared App URL at the top right of the builder to launch in a new tab where microphone popups work instantly!
